Measurement method

Average Temperature =

Sensor anomaly detection

Readings can naturally differ because conditions may vary by location. A sensor should not be called defective simply because its reading differs. Future detection should identify persistent or clearly abnormal readings compared with the other sensors.

SCHOOL SCIENCE & INNOVATION PROJECT

Smart Grain Storage Monitoring System

A transparent prototype exploring how multi-point environmental measurements can support earlier, better-informed storage inspections.

PROBLEM

Post-harvest grains may experience unfavorable environmental conditions during storage. Changes in temperature and relative humidity can increase the risk of deterioration and create conditions that require inspection.

OBJECTIVE

To monitor temperature and relative humidity at multiple locations and provide an early warning when conditions move outside configured monitoring limits.

HYPOTHESIS

If conditions are monitored at multiple locations, changes can be identified earlier than relying only on periodic manual inspection.
